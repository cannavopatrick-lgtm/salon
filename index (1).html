“La Padellina”

La Tradizione di Roma

Handcrafted Daily, Two Minutes from Piazza San Pietro.

Since 1876, our family kitchen has honored the soul of Roman gastronomy. Our square-cut tonnarelli are extruded fresh every morning using bronze dies and stone-ground semolina so every strand clings to rich sauces.

Whether you are stepping out of the Vatican Museums for lunch or gathering with friends under the warm lights of Via del Mascherino for dinner, every guest is welcomed like family—including vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free diners.
